Hibiscus Coral Inn is a small inn on Bantayan Island, off the north-west coast
of Cebu. There are a handful of rooms and the sea is a short walk away. It is a
place to stay for a few quiet days, or a few weeks.

Days here are slow and unstructured: swim while the water is still flat, eat,
sleep through the heat of the afternoon, walk back to the beach before the sun
goes down. Nobody will hand you an itinerary.

We answer enquiries ourselves, usually within a day. If you want to know
whether something is possible, ask — the answer is often yes, and if it is no,
we will say so.
